A lightweight version control system

MiniVCS tracks your files, saves snapshots, supports branches and merging, and lets teams collaborate on shared repositories.

Create an account or sign in to start.

What you can do

  • Create public or private repositories.
  • Upload files, stage them and commit snapshots.
  • Create branches, switch between them and merge them.
  • Browse commit history and manage snapshot retention.
  • Invite collaborators with owner, maintainer, contributor or viewer roles.
